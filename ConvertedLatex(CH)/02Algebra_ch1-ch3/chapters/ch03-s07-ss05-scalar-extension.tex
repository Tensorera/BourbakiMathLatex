命题 8. 设 A、B 为两个交换环， \(\rho: A \to B\) 一个环同态，且 M 是一个 A-模。典范扩张

\[
\psi : \bigwedge_ {\mathbf {B}} (\mathbf {B} \otimes_ {\mathbf {A}} \mathbf {M}) \rightarrow \mathbf {B} \otimes_ {\mathbf {A}} \bigwedge_ {\mathbf {A}} (\mathbf {M})
\]
