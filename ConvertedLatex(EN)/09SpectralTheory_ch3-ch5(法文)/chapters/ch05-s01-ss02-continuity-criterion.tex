\subsection{A criterion for continuity}

The following result makes it possible to verify that certain linear representations of a direct product of topological groups are continuous.

Lemma 1. --- Let G and H be topological groups and E a Banach space. Let \(\varrho\) be a bounded representation of G in E and \(\pi\) a bounded representation of H in E. Suppose that \(\varrho(g)\) commutes with \(\pi(h)\) for all \((g,h)\in\mathrm{G}\times\mathrm{H}\) . The map \(\varpi\) of G \(\times\) H in \(\mathbf{GL}(\mathrm{E})\) defined by \((g,h)\mapsto\varrho(g)\circ\pi(h)\) is a continuous and bounded linear representation of G \(\times\) H in E.

The map \(\varpi\) is a linear representation of G × H in E; let us verify that it is continuous. Since \(\| \varpi(g, h) \| \leqslant \| \varrho(g) \| \| \pi(h) \|\) for all \((g, h) \in \mathrm{G} \times \mathrm{H}\) , the representation \(\varpi\) is bounded, hence its image is equicontinuous in \(\mathcal{L}(\mathrm{E})\) . It then suffices to show that, for every \(x \in \mathrm{E}\) , the map \((g, h) \mapsto \varpi(g, h)x\) is continuous (remark 2 of INT, VIII, p. 129, § 2, no. 1). Let \((g_0, h_0) \in \mathrm{G} \times \mathrm{H}\) . Set \(y = \pi(h_0)x\) . For every \((g, h) \in \mathrm{G} \times \mathrm{H}\) , it follows that

\[
\begin{array}{r l} & {\| \varpi (g, h) x - \varpi (g _ {0}, h _ {0}) x \| \leqslant \| \varrho (g) (\pi (h) x - y) \| + \| \varrho (g) y - \varrho (g _ {0}) y \|} \\ & {\qquad \leqslant \| \varrho (g) \| \| \pi (h) x - y \| + \| \varrho (g) y - \varrho (g _ {0}) y \|.} \end{array}
\]

Since \(\varrho\) is bounded and that \(\varrho\) and \(\pi\) are continuous, this implies the assertion.
