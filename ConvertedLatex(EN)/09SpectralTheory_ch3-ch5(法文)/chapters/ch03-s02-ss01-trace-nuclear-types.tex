\subsection{\texorpdfstring{Endomorphisms of finite trace, Hilbert--Schmidt, and nuclear \(p^{e}\)-power type}{Endomorphisms of finite trace, Hilbert--Schmidt, and nuclear p\^{}\{e\}-power type}}

*Let E and F be Hilbert spaces. Every endomorphism of E of finite trace (EVT, V, p. 49, def. 7 and p. 50, def. 8) is compact (IV, p. 165, cor. 1); every Hilbert--Schmidt map from E to F (EVT, V, p. 51, def. 9) is compact (IV, p. 165, cor. 2).

For every real number \(p \geqslant 1\) , the vector space \(\mathcal{L}_{p}(\mathrm{E};\mathrm{F})\) of linear maps from E to F of nuclear \(p^{\mathrm{e}}\)-power type is contained in \(\mathcal{L}^{\mathrm{c}}(\mathrm{E};\mathrm{F})\) (cf. IV, p. 169, remark 2 for \(p = 1\) ).*
