¶ 16) Let X, X' be two metrizable spaces. A mapping \(f: \mathbf{X} \to \mathbf{X}'\) is said to be a Borel mapping if, for each closed subset F' of X', \(f^{-1}(\mathbf{F}')\) is a Borel set in X.
